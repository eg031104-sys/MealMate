## Why the target was not met

Direct official API download failed at the workspace proxy (CONNECT 403). Some official API responses were available through the web research tool, but not a complete reliable candidate collection. The API does not supply a standard recipe serving-yield field. Missing yields cannot be assumed to be four. Unmeasured ingredients, unspecified cans/packets, unknown cup conventions, incomplete directions and quantity conflicts need rejection or primary-source confirmation. A local 418-file public-domain source collection was scanned for photos/yields and candidate ingredient lists, then selected candidates were manually checked; this was not a claim that all 418 passed human review.

Rejected candidates are not automatically added as normal recipes, silently assigned guessed quantities, or marked ready. Existing review entries, archived entries, favourites and household imports are preserved. Only missing, non-duplicate recipe records are appended; a household-edited/archived recipe is never overwritten or restored by this pack.

Only two ready dinner recipes exist in the shipped genuine collection after this change (the old slow-cooker recipe plus Sticky Chicken). Seven distinct dinners therefore cannot be generated from the bundled collection alone. More independently validated recipes or household-created/imported recipes are required. The planner does not invent replacements to conceal this shortfall.
